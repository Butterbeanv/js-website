Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Bloxburg Certification collects, how it is used, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use Bloxburg Certification, we may collect information necessary to provide the service, such as your username, email address, account information, test results, and information you choose to add to your profile.

We only use this information for purposes related to operating, maintaining, and improving Bloxburg Certification.

2. Service Providers

Bloxburg Certification uses third-party service providers to help operate the website. These services may process or store information necessary to provide the service, including account and authentication information.

Bloxburg Certification currently uses Supabase for authentication and database services and Render for website hosting.

3. We Will Never Sell Your Information

Your information is not for sale.

Bloxburg Certification will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers, companies, or other organizations.

4. Test Scores

Test scores and certification results are stored securely and are used for certification and administrative purposes.

Access to test results is limited to authorized personnel who need the information to operate or administer the certification system.

5. Password Security

Passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication rather than being stored as readable passwords by Bloxburg Certification staff.

Staff, owners, and other members of the organization do not have access to your plaintext password through the Bloxburg Certification website.

You should never share your password with another person, including staff members or administrators.

6. Account Information

We do not intentionally make private account information available to other users.

You are responsible for keeping your login information secure and for notifying an administrator if you believe your account has been compromised.

7. Discord

Bloxburg Certification may provide Discord links for community, support, or administrative communication.

Discord is a separate service and has its own privacy policy and terms. Information you provide directly to Discord is handled according to Discord's policies.

8. Profile Pictures

If you choose to add a custom profile picture, the image may be loaded from the image URL you provide.

Do not use an image that you do not have permission to use, and do not upload or link to inappropriate or private information.

9. Staff Access

Authorized staff may have access to information necessary to operate the certification system, administer tests, assist users, and resolve technical or administrative issues.

Staff should only access information when it is necessary for their role.

10. Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored by the service. However, no online service can guarantee that data transmitted or stored online will always be completely secure.

11. Data Deletion

If you would like your account or personal information deleted, you may contact the Bloxburg Certification technical support team. Requests will be reviewed and handled as appropriate for the operation of the certification system.

12. Contact

If you have a technical problem with your account or the website, contact the Lowery Academy Marketing & Tech Specialist.

For non-technical concerns, contact Mr. Louie, the Lead & Main Trust Leader.
